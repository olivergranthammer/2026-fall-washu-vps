The Forbidden Zone

I agree with Damon's contention that creating a container for your art (whether it is physical or digital) is a great way to inspire yourself to make art, as you will gain the compulsion to fill your container and create an artistic narrative for yourself. I love his practice of getting texts in the morning showing him something he was inspired by in the past, as I think a lot of what people collect can get buried and forgotten. I think this applies to myself with my personal portfolio creation as an architecture student. The fact that I have a portfolio makes me excited to create new things and develop an artistic personality for myself that I can share with others. However, I enjoy collecting physical things more than digital things, as it feels more "real" to me. This is probably because I grew up overly saturated on the internet and now feel like anything I put on there is owned by a larger corporation. Now, I am collecting CDs, dvds, and physical art pieces and they feel so much more valuable to me, like I actually own it even though I didn't necessarily create it.
