My Website it a Shifting House

I really liked 1. The categorization of websites as an artform 2. The use of curation/ creation of metaphors to describe the different possibilities of what a website can be and 3. The rejection of depicting the web as a “cloud”. This essay really homes in on the importance of websites as a form of expression and gives the power back to the small website creators that imbed their personality in their work, rather than corporations which prioritize ad revenue. If I had a website, it would be akin to a sculpture garden; a place where you can aimlessly wander through “nature” and stumble upon beautiful works of art on your path. So, basically an interactive portfolio with hidden links and other fun immersive pathways, so the website becomes a sort of walking museum. Something that I can make myself that wouldn't be possible if someone else made my website would be encapsulating the aesthetic/purpose of my work within the interface design as to be homogenous with my work. If someone else tried this, they wouldn't understand the mindset I had during creation.
