I personally agree with Toph Tucker’s opinion that older websites from the 1990s and 2000s (hand-coded with HTML rather than built on templates) have a distinct personality and aesthetic which is missing from today's standardized web. My preference is most likely influenced by a certain nostalgia for the old web, but there is something inherently comforting/welcoming about a website which was essentially crafted by hand. Like an old home, each design choice that was made throughout the coding gives it a story and reflects the person who curated it. On the other hand, HTML is very restrictive in its interface, so if someone wanted to make a more immersive website, something like CSS would be better in that case.
