Eno

This was a fantastic and utterly creative film. I love Brain Eno’s artistic process and his ability to resist latching onto artistic practices (especially within music) that already exist, and rather create things which value lies in the genuine emotional experience of it. His process with generative music is fascinating, and how he describes it as planting the seed for something and letting it grow on its own volition. His example with the synth that takes the notes you give it and chooses to repeat different notes on each loop is a great example of this. I believe that Eno is a musical painter rather than a traditional artist; he uses everything and sees musical value in everything, especially things that are overlooked such as sampling radio interviews and recordings of nature itself. I personally love ambient music and didn't realize that Eno literally invented it in the 70’s. He sees a potential for beauty in anything and a lot of what he does feels like a decision humanity made in a different universe or timeline.
