Human-Made Creation

The human-made creation that brings out the best in me is music. I love to create music, play music (guitar) and listen to music all the time. I believe it to be one of the purest artforms ever developed; when you think of it, it's just the organization of sound that comes together to form emotions. It takes one human sense, hearing, and manipulates it in varying ways to communicate things that cannot be done through basic language. The creation of music calls fourth the human desire to communicate emotion. Interestingly, it's the only form of art to my knowledge that is used by animals as well. Making music, and art in general, to me is all about taking risks and exposing your most hidden memories, opinions, and feelings. This is why I don't consider AI music to BE music. Taking a dataset of people's feelings and emotions depicted in sound and creating an amalgamation of it is the exact opposite of taking risk and the antithesis of communicating a personal story. In terms of creating and playing music, there are many aspects of it that I enjoy, and aspects that are uncomfortable as well. Will people like what I write? Will people judge me for it? But these questions of uncertainty make me feel more human than anything else and instill that much more power into what I write.
