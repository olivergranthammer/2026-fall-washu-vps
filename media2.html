There are no Gardens on the Internet

The first thing this taught me was that the idea of a "website as a game" is possible and the possibilities for something like that are vast. In all, this site was a beautifully surreal experience. The art style (I'm not even sure how to describe it) with very high contrast, almost psychedelic colors were otherworldly, along with the soundscapes on each page and the sound effects that accompanied them when clicking on things. I loved the idea of having an "inventory" where you can collect things and eventually use them on other pages. I want to incorporate this into my next website, where I want to do either a maze or a museum. The progress bar on the bottom was also nice, as it showed how many things you experienced on the page, however I think it was a little too handholdey. All in all, this gave me many ideas I want to incorporate into my next website, and I'll be visiting this again to understand how these things were coded.
