The Coming Age of Calm Technology

For an article written in the late 90's, these predictions for "calm" technology which integrates seamlessly within our lives are surprisingly accurate. We have smart fridges, Alexas, and other computers that are intertwined with other aspects of our everyday lives. However, these predictions are missing the fact that humans crave constant excitement and stimulation, even when it's harmful in the long term. With the implementation of social media in our lives, we expect a perfect catering of media to our tastes, and with the development of chatbots and generative AI, people are beginning to rely more on computers rather than calmly integrating with them. One thing that Brown correctly predicted, however, is that some people will become sick of this dependence and seek more "real" human experiences that reject the requirement of computers.
