What do you want to learn in this course?

Through taking this class, I want to use the skills of HTML to fabricate a public website for my architecture portfolio. I also want to learn more about the intersection between website creation and graphic design.

Tell us about an early experience with computers or the internet

The first experience I've had with a computer that I can remember was in Kindergarden. I was at the computer lab at my school, and we were learning the different things computers could do. My favorite program by far was MS Paint. I loved how cool the different tools were and felt like I had no limits to what I could create.

What will the web look like in 10 years from now?

I beleive that the web will go through a aesthetic-oriented rennaisance throughout the next ten years. This will be in resistance to the corperate minimalism that is so common in todays company websites. Also, with the popularization of AI, website design will be more radical and unique in order for website designers to differenciate themselves.
