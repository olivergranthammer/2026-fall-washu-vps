Ritual

One ritual / practice in my life that's extremely important to my psychological fulfillment are the practices for my powerlifting club. 3 times a week, my team goes to an industrial gym outside of campus called the LAB and we practice powerlifting there. Not only is this important for my health in terms of physical exercise, but it also is vital for my mental health. Being around people who share the same interest as I do and having them push me to my limits (and I do to them) gives me social and psychological fulfillment. The environment is also important too, as it pulls me out of my comfort zone (home, studio, etc.) and places me somewhere where there is a lot of people, screaming, banging of weights, and loud music. It's uncomfortable sometimes but I think discomfort is the only way one can reach true fulfillment. In my opinion, being in a stasis of comfort is how people become stuck and don't put themselves out there, as I know this has happened to me. Overall, being on this team and having the practices as a "ritual" has improved my life, introduced me to some of my best friends, and taught me a lot about the benefits of putting myself in unfamiliar / uncomfortable settings.
