When do you cherish the difficult way?

One activity that I love to do even though it can be inefficient or difficult is being the DM for my D&D campaign. As the dungeon master, you have to plan very well, be a leader, and think on your feet a lot of the time. As an introvert, these tasks are difficult and uncomfortable sometimes, however there are moments of extreme fun and adrenaline when I take this role. For example, when the players do something that I haven't thought of, I must fabricate a consequence or effect that occurs in real time very quickly. This can be stressful, but it's also the beauty of D&D; collective storytelling. It's a great way to put myself out of my comfort zone and create a story that my players will enjoy and feel as though they are the creators of their own destiny. DMing has taught me a lot and helped me develop skills to become a more confident, quick-thinking person. The easier way to play dnd in my mind is being the player rather than the DM, and both of these roles are vastly different experiences, but I choose to DM to build skills and create deeper connections with my friends. Before proposing this campaign to my friends, I was quite nervous as to whether they would enjoy it. But I took a leap and made it happen. Even though not every session is engaging and smooth, the overall story has become very important to me and my group.
